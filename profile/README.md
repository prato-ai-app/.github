# Prato AI

## Hi there 👋

"Prato" means *meadow* in Italian. 🌾 An open field. 🌱 A place where things grow — quietly, seasonally, without being forced.

That's what we want food technology to feel like. Not a lab. Not a spreadsheet. A meadow. ✨

Prato AI is created not to replace the joy of cooking or turn dinner into data — but to *understand* it. To see what's growing on the plate.

### 🌻 What we believe

- 🌾 Good things grow — recipes, skills, tastes. They don't appear fully formed.
- 🍽️ Food is more than fuel — it's memory, culture, care, and craft.
- 📸 A meal is a whole story — what it looks like, what's in it, how it was made, what it gives you.
- 💛 Recipes should live and breathe — not sit frozen on a webpage full of ads.
- 🌍 What you cook should fit *your* life — your kitchen, your tastes, your leftovers.

### 🌱 What we're growing

- 👁️ **Food understanding** — machines that actually *see* a meal, not just label it.
- 🔄 **Reverse recipe generation** — snap a photo, get a recipe back. From the dish, back to the method.
- 🧠 **Nutrition that means something** — connected to real food, real portions, real people.
- 📖 **Recipes that behave** — generate, scale, swap, adapt, personalize.
- 🤖 **Food agents** — ones that know your meals, your history, your kitchen.
